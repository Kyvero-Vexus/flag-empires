* Style Bible of the Banner Annals

** Purpose
This codex governs diction, cadence, chronology, and referential method for the Flag-Empires corpus. The tone is high chronicle, grave but lucid, with periodic lyrical expansion. Every chapter shall move between three registers: annal, witness, and lament.

** Core Register
- Prefer long, rolling sentences for historical sweep.
- Break into short, hard clauses for battle, famine, betrayal, and decree.
- Keep names consistent by canonical form.
- Use archaic flavor sparingly; clarity is law.

** Temporal Architecture
The history is divided into numbered Ages and Year-Counts.
- DA: Dawn Age, before imperial calendars.
- FA: First Age of Raising Banners.
- SA: Second Age of Salt and Coin.
- TA: Third Age of Iron Roads.
- LA: Last Age, ending in the Last Year.

All narrative events must include a Canon ID and at least one date mark in one of these systems.

** Canon ID Syntax
Format: FE-<AGE>-<BOOK>-<SERIES>-<ITEM>
Example: FE-SA-B2-ANN-044
Where:
- AGE: DA, FA, SA, TA, LA
- BOOK: B0 (meta), B1, B2, B3
- SERIES: ANN (annal), EDI (edict), WIT (witness), MAP, LIN, RIT
- ITEM: three-digit index

** Required Sections per Major Chapter
1. Heading with year-span.
2. Annals snippet block.
3. Narrative prose.
4. Optional witness fragments.
5. Canon references list.

** Annals Snippet Form
Use fixed pattern:
#+begin_quote
[Canon: FE-SA-B2-ANN-044 | Year: SA 233]
In the month of westward dust the Fifth Banner crossed the Dry Seam, and no bell was rung in greeting.
#+end_quote

** On Peoples and Polities
Avoid caricature. Every polity must contain factions, internal memory, and contradictory virtues.

** Lexicon Standards
- Use “Banner-Lord” for supreme military ruler.
- Use “Seal-Keeper” for civil archivist authority.
- Use “Mast-City” for fortified capital.
- Use “Salt Courts” for mercantile tribunals.
- Use “Road-Wardens” for imperial logistics corps.

** Refrains and Recurring Images
Approved motifs:
- Cloth in wind as omen of legitimacy.
- Salt as covenant and corruption.
- Lantern smoke as persistence of memory.
- River mouths as thresholds of empire.

** Prohibitions
- No placeholder markers.
- No modern slang.
- No unexplained retcons.
- No chapter without at least three Canon IDs.

** Editorial Coherence Rules
- When uncertain between two dates, record both in annal dispute note.
- Distinguish between archive fact and witness belief.
- Preserve contradictory testimony; reconcile only with evidence.

** Pronunciation and Orthography Guide
- Flagartha: FLAH-gar-tha.
- Flagistan: FLAH-jis-tan.
- Surfacers: SUR-fay-sers.
- Kharad Tines: KHA-rad TEENS.

** End Matter Standard
Each book ends with:
- Roll of reigning houses.
- Disputed entries.
- Losses uncounted.
